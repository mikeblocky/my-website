I started drawing for fun, with a simple goal: to create a piece celebrating my favorite artist’s birthday. With his encouragement, I kept going, drawing more and eventually sharing my work publicly on social media. At that time, I was drawing for myself, my own characters, ideas, and stories.

Then I discovered *Skip and Loafer*, and for nearly three years, I consistently created art for it, along with occasional fan works for other series: Kemutai Hanashi, Stars Align, etc.

It has been a meaningful journey. Being part of a community and connecting with people I never expected to meet gave me the motivation to keep drawing. For that, I am grateful—to everyone, and to you.

Beyond drawing, I write about characters, translate interviews I enjoy, and build personal websites. In a way, it feels like there is nothing I cannot do. Yet at the same time, I feel empty. Much of what I have done comes from a desire to be seen, rather than a clear understanding of myself. I realize now that I have not spent enough time reflecting on who I am or what I truly want.

I enjoy being around others, but I hesitate to form deeper connections. I am afraid of causing pain—to myself or to others. Even when I try to hold onto connections gently, they are difficult to maintain. Being forgotten is something I still fear, even though I know it is inevitable.

I have tried many ways to feel connected, to belong. But over time, repeated rejection has made it harder to continue. It hurts more than I expected, and I no longer know how to keep going in the same way.

For now, stepping back feels necessary. I will still continue some things, but I plan to reduce my presence on social media and focus more on my life outside of it.

I may stop drawing, or I may not. I am not sure yet, but that is okay for me. Right now, I just need some distance.

Thank you for reading.

I hope we may meet again someday, in a different way.
