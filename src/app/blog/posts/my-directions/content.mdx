I started drawing for fun with a purpose to create a piece to celebrate my favorite artist’s birthday. With his encouragement, I kept drawing more, and eventually started sharing my works publicly on social media. At that time, I drew for myself, with my own characters, ideas, and stories.

Then I started watching Skip and Loafer and began drawing for it consistently for almost three years, along with some fan art for other series over time.

It has been a meaningful journey to be part of a community and to connect with people I never thought I would meet. Because of them, and because of you, I have been able to keep my motivation to draw until now.

Besides drawing, I also write my own stories and thoughts about characters, translate interviews I like, and build personal websites. I feel like there is nothing I cannot do, but at the same time I feel empty. What I have been doing often comes from a hope to be seen, rather than something I truly understand about myself. I realize now that I have not spent enough time thinking about who I am or what I want.

I enjoy being around others, but I hesitate to go too deep into connections because I am afraid of hurting myself or others. I have experienced that kind of pain before. Even when I try to hold onto connections gently, I find it difficult to keep them. The idea of being forgotten is something I am still afraid of, even though I know it is inevitable.

For now, I think stepping back is the direction I need. I will still continue some of the things I usually do, but I plan to reduce my presence on social media. I want to focus more on my life outside of it instead of continuing in the same way and waiting for something to change.

I may stop drawing, or I may not continue the things I have been doing until now. I am not sure yet, and that is okay. For now, I just want to step away.

Thank you for reading.
I hope we may meet again someday, in a different way.
