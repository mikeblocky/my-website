# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Creative individuals, friends, developers, and visitors interested in personal illustrations, Japanese literature, blogging, and quiet reflection.

## Product Purpose
A personal digital archive and a quiet, slow space to showcase illustration works, publish written diaries and essays, and connect with people in a thoughtful manner.

## Brand Personality
Quiet, soft, minimalist, reflective, personal.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS dashboards, card-based marketing templates, loud text gradients, heavy drop shadows, over-designed boxes, and dense layouts.

## Design Principles
1. **Simple and Quiet**: The layout should breathe and give full center stage to the artworks.
2. **Textured and Soft**: Colors and backgrounds should feel organic, soft, and warm.
3. **Intentional Motion**: Micro-interactions should mimic physical weight and ease gently rather than drawing attention to themselves.
4. **Honest Typography**: Clean, legible typography with a clear hierarchy that doesn't feel cramped or loud.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Contrast ratios >= 4.5:1 for body copy.
- Full support for system-wide light/dark modes.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all animations.
